About

I build products that actually matter to people, and I lead the teams that build them, turning messy, complicated problems into clean solutions that scale.

Fifteen years in, I still write code most weeks. But the job now is the shape of the whole system: two development teams, one quality engineering team, and a software development lifecycle being rebuilt around AI tooling from planning through review.

Before management I was a tech lead, a UI developer, and for a while a one-person design studio. That history shows up in how I run teams: I care about what ships, how it looks, and whether anyone can trust it.
